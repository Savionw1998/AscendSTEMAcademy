When you order, you’ll be asked to:

  1. List the relevant course names and final grades

  3. Choose delivery by mail or emailed PDF

Student and Educator IDs

Because who doesn’t love flashing a badge? These IDs work for enrollment verification and student discounts. The Educator ID can be used for discounts at retailers including Michael’s, Office Depot, and Barnes & Noble.

Your Educator ID and/or a Teacher Verification Letter (a letter from Ascend confirming you are your enrolled student’s home educator) can also be submitted to ID.me to unlock further discounts on theme parks, hotels, car rentals, flights, and shopping.

Each ID features a 1-inch by 1-inch photo headshot. After ordering, you’ll be prompted to upload a photo of the student or educator and tell us exactly how the name should appear.

Cost per ID: $25Delivered in 5–7 business days.
