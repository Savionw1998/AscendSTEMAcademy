FLVS Flex Program

Ascend STEM Academy partners with Florida Virtual School (FLVS) Flex to provide students with a personalized and flexible learning experience. This collaboration allows students to take advantage of FLVS Flex’s self-paced online courses while Ascend STEM Academy keeps their records and provides guidance and one-on-one support.

Ascend STEM Academy and College of Central Florida Dual Enrollment Partnership

Ascend STEM Academy is proud to partner with the College of Central Florida (CF) to offer an exceptional dual enrollment program for our ambitious high school students. This partnership empowers students to jumpstart their college education while pursuing a strong foundation in STEM disciplines.
