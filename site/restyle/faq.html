The basics

Umbrella schools and the law

What is an “Umbrella School”?

An “Umbrella School” is a term for non-campus based private schools like Ascend STEM Academy. These schools support homeschooling families and are sometimes referred to as “cover schools” because they provide legal coverage for homeschooling.

What is a “600 School”?

A “600 School” refers to Florida private schools that offer a homeschooling option. The term “600” refers to state statutes governing private school incorporation. Legally, there is no difference between a private school that offers a homeschooling option and one that does not.

May I enroll my child in a private school and still homeschool?

Absolutely, as long as the private school offers a non-campus or homeschool option like Ascend STEM Academy.

If I enroll my child in a private school, must I notify the superintendent?

No, you don’t file anything with the superintendent yourself. Once your child is enrolled with Ascend, they are a private school student rather than a home education student, and Ascend files the required notice with your school district as their school of record. Are all “umbrella schools” basically the same?

Not at all! Each school has its unique personality, procedures, and requirements. Key differentiators include:

  • Courses Taught - Does the school require a specific curriculum or can parents choose their child’s coursework?
  • Philosophy - Does the school have a particular educational or religious philosophy that aligns with your family’s values and goals?
  • Support - Does the school offer the level of support your family needs? Do you prefer more structure or more freedom with available support?
  • Record Keeping - Does the school handle recordkeeping for you? What must you report to the school and how often? Is the reporting system easy to comply with?
  • Anything else that is important to your family! Be sure to reach out to school personnel to discuss how the school may or may not align with your family’s needs and goals.
Day to day

Curriculum, attendance and the school year

Is there a specific curriculum required?

No. Each student at Ascend STEM Academy is led by their home educator, usually a parent. Home educators are free to use the curriculum of their choosing and change it when needed.

Will my child need to take standardized testing?

We do not mandate that our students take any form of standardized testing.

Is my child able to enroll in Florida Virtual School?

Yes! Ascend STEM Academy students can take free FLVS Flex courses, with hundreds of options for grades K–12. We’ll send registration details in your Parent-Teacher Manual when you enroll.

When does the school year for Ascend STEM Academy start?

A student may enroll at any time. The school year for each student starts on the day of his or her enrollment and lasts 365 days. The next school year starts on the anniversary of his or her enrollment.

How many days of school are required?

Ascend students complete 180 days of instruction per school year. That means 180 school days, not calendar days. Track them as you go on your time card.

How is attendance collected?

Home educators should track attendance daily, but it’s only collected at the end of March, June, September, and December. Quarterly reminders of attendance deadlines are sent via our newsletter. Attendance is collected electronically.

Can my child dual enroll at the College of Central Florida or another local college?

Yes, they can. The College of Central Florida is the main option for families in Marion, Citrus and Levy counties. Families in other areas can ask their local state college about dual enrollment for private school students. See our Resources page for details.

Is an Ascend STEM Academy diploma different from other umbrella schools?

Our diplomas are structured to mirror Florida’s state academic standards, so your student’s transcript looks familiar and credible to colleges, employers, and scholarship committees rather than like an informal homeschool record.

Enrolling and leaving

Scholarships, switching and withdrawing

Can we use a Florida scholarship like Step Up For Students?

Scholarship rules differ by program and change from year to year. Read our guide, Understanding Florida School Choice Scholarships and Florida Umbrella Schools, or contact us to talk through your situation. We can provide an enrollment verification letter if your program asks for one.

Can we switch to Ascend in the middle of the school year?

Yes. Enrollment is open year-round, and your child’s Ascend school year starts the day you enroll. If your child is in public school now, the withdrawal letter generator on our Enrollment page writes the notice for your current school.

Ascend STEM Academy isn’t the right fit. Can we withdraw?

Yes. Simply contact us via email to terminate enrollment. Your withdrawal will be recorded on the date that your request was sent. Ascend STEM Academy does not offer refunds if a student withdraws before his or her school year is completed. A student who withdraws from Ascend STEM Academy can enroll in public or private school or begin a course of homeschool.
