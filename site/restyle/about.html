Our mission

A supportive and flexible education framework

At Ascend STEM Academy, our mission is to empower students and families through a supportive and flexible education framework. We prioritize STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) learning alongside preparation for skilled trades. We are dedicated to fostering a dynamic learning environment where curiosity, innovation, and hands-on experience are paramount.

While we do not provide a specific curriculum, we offer resources, guidance, and support to help families and students create personalized educational paths that lead to academic excellence and successful careers in trades. Our goal is to nurture a diverse and inclusive community where every student can thrive, discover their passions, and become leaders in their chosen fields equipped with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to succeed in a rapidly evolving world.

Who we are

What we stand for

  1. Vision and Values

    We envision a future where every student has the opportunity to excel academically and professionally in STEM and skilled trades. Our core values of integrity, innovation, and excellence guide everything we do, ensuring a supportive and enriching educational experience for all.

  2. Commitment to Excellence

    We are dedicated to continuous improvement and strive for excellence in all aspects of our educational programs and services. We uphold high standards of educational quality and are committed to meeting or exceeding your family’s expectations.

  3. Support and Guidance

    We value the partnership between school and family in fostering student success. We offer a range of support services, including guides, project ideas, and college and career support, to ensure every student receives the support they need to thrive.

  4. Educational Approach

    Our school embraces a flexible education framework that allows families to tailor learning experiences to meet individual student needs. We integrate STEM education with hands-on learning opportunities in trades to prepare students for real-world challenges and opportunities.

  5. Community and Diversity

    We are committed to creating an inclusive and supportive school community where diversity is celebrated. We encourage students to explore their interests, develop leadership skills, and contribute positively to society.

  6. Curriculum and Resources

    While we do not prescribe a specific curriculum, we provide comprehensive resources and guidance to support families in selecting educational materials that align with their educational goals.
