High School Graduation Requirements

Ascend STEM Academy is here to help! To ensure our high school diplomas remain competitive, we align our graduation requirements with Florida state standards. Ascend STEM Academy students must achieve a 2.5 GPA to graduate. Upon completing the high school diploma requirements described below, Ascend students must apply to graduate using our Graduation Application Form.

  • Forensic Science
  • Marine Science 1
  • Natural Resources and Conservation
  • Physical Science
  • Physics

Electives

Students entering high school in August 2023 or later must earn 7.5 credits in electives. Students who entered high school before August 2023 must earn 8 credits in electives. Here are some sample electives:

  • Computer Science and Coding
  • Engineering and Robotics
  • World Languages (Spanish, ASL and more)
  • Psychology
  • Economics
  • Art, Music or Theatre
  • Career and Technical Education courses
  • Extra math and science courses (for example Chemistry or Physics)
