Ascend STEM Academy Enrollment Agreement

I, the parent/legal guardian of the student identified on this Enrollment Form, agree to the following terms and conditions in order to enroll my child at Ascend STEM Academy:

This agreement covers

  • Educational Commitment

Educational Commitment

  1. To provide instruction for the required 180 days per year and the necessary number of hours per grade level.
  2. To submit a quarterly report of daily attendance to the school administration, and to fulfill all requirements documented in the Policies section of the Welcome Packet or otherwise requested by Ascend STEM Academy.
  3. To maintain accurate records of courses taken, achievements therein (including date of graduation), and fulfill any other requirements of the State of Florida as related to private schools (Section 1002.42, Florida Statutes).

Tuition and Financial Responsibility

To pay tuition as outlined on the Enrollment Form.

Liability and Legal Compliance

I release Ascend STEM Academy from any and all responsibility and absolve them from any claim of loss, damage, or injury of any nature to person or property resulting from the school program. I also agree that Ascend STEM Academy shall not be liable for any loss or intentional neglect or careless acts of any school personnel.

I understand that failure to comply with these policies is cause for withdrawal from Ascend STEM Academy and for our records to be withheld.

I agree to follow the laws and regulations regarding the education of my child, as set forth by the State of Florida and, if applicable, my local educational laws and regulations.

Parental Responsibility

All details given in this form to Ascend STEM Academy are accurate and truthful. Ascend STEM Academy is permitted to use and store this information to maintain my child’s official school records.

I will not hold Ascend STEM Academy liable for my educational decisions and agree to be responsible for exercising my personal discretion in following any advice given by Ascend STEM Academy.

By checking the agreement box and submitting my enrollment, I agree to the terms stated above and wish to officially enroll my child as a student of Ascend STEM Academy.
